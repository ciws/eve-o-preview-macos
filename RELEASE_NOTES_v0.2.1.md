# EVE-O Preview for macOS v0.2.1

This update improves thumbnail dragging, Light Mode readability, and switching between EVE clients.

- Drag previews smoothly and save their positions on release. A click still activates the matching client.
- Keep the selected client in front without switching back to the first-launched client.
- Use readable panel colours in both Light and Dark Mode.

Download the Apple Silicon DMG attached to this release.

The app is ad hoc signed and is not notarized by Apple. Accessibility, Automation, and Screen Recording permissions may be needed.

The Release build, app signature, and DMG integrity were checked locally. Live testing with logged-in EVE clients is still pending.
